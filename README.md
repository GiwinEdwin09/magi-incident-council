# MAGI: Incident Council

An incident council of three AI agents with competing mandates (restore service, contain harm, protect customers). They negotiate what to do during production incidents, act on their own when they agree on something reversible, and phone a human only when they genuinely disagree about something irreversible.

Built for the UCSC AI Hackathon (Oct 10–11, 2026), Cisco Outshift / AGNTCY Cloud Track, Level 3 "Build a Collective Intelligence."
